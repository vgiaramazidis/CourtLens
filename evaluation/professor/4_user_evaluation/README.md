# User evaluation

Completed user-testing results are available for ten pseudonymous participants. All nine tasks were recorded as successful for every participant. The study owner confirmed that participants completed the tasks independently.

- [CourtLens_User_Evaluation.xlsx](results/CourtLens_User_Evaluation.xlsx): English-only workbook with participant ratings, task outcomes, participant comments, reported themes and recalculated statistics.
- [REPORT.md](REPORT.md): study findings, source definitions and limitations.

The Excel workbook is the data source cited by both CourtLens reports. Its Tasks and Ratings sheets define the completed nine-task, five-rating study. This folder contains the completed study and its results.

Session dates, recruitment, demographics, device allocation, task timings and retries were not supplied. The workbook preserves recorded values and labels missing study information explicitly. Do not treat the mean divided by five as a percentage of satisfied participants or a validated usability score.
