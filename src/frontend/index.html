    BASKETBALL TERMINOLOGY

    Glossary

    Assist
    A pass that directly leads to a teammate scoring.
    Turnover
    Losing possession of the ball to the other team.
    Rebound
    Recovering the ball after a missed shot.
    Second-chance points
    Points scored after recovering your own team’s missed shot.
    Fast break
    A quick attack before the defense has time to get into position.
    Lineup
    The five players on court together. Plus/minus is the difference between their team’s points scored and conceded.
    Game clock
    The time left in a period. Q4, 02:14 means fourth quarter, 2 minutes 14 seconds remaining. OT means overtime.
    Positions
    Guards handle the ball and create chances: point guards (PG) often lead the offense, while shooting guards (SG) focus on scoring. Forwards play on the wings and near the basket: small forwards (SF) combine scoring and defense, and power forwards (PF) often rebound and defend inside. Centers (C) usually play closest to the basket. Players can cover more than one position.
